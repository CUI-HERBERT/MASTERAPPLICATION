## Boundaries for programme scoring

The seed intentionally carries no invented score, prestige multiplier, Q1 flag, or acceptance probability. A paper title/count, an award, a leadership post or a high-status recommender cannot alone move a programme tier. Contributions, completed status, relevance, specific recommendation evidence, controlled repeated experimental results and demonstrated independent understanding remain separate. Existing ML publications must not be converted into experimental electrochemistry/materials outputs.
